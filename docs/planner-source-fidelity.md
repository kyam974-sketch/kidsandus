# Planner source-fidelity contract

This is a blocking import/validation contract for canonical Kids&Us Planner lessons.

1. **Teacher Guide is the source of truth.** Planner notes may compress explanatory prose, but may not invent, normalize or remove teacher/student actions that change how an activity is delivered.
2. **Mandatory speech is lossless.** Every phrase the Teacher Guide instructs the teacher or children to say/repeat/chant/ask/answer must appear in Planner notes verbatim and be wrapped in `**...**` so the UI renders it bold. This includes requests such as `Can I ... please?`, model answers, chants, counting, Kan-do dialogue, workbook handout language, game language and Goodbye language.
3. **Routines are expanded.** A Planner activity may not contain only labels such as `Hello + Register`, `Weather`, `Moods`, `Looking for Sam`, etc. It must expand the exact routine/version specified by that course/story/day, including mandatory speech and operational actions.
4. **HOW TO PLAY is preserved operationally.** Keep Before Class, Warm-up, Main Activity, KEY/target structures, optional branches that affect delivery, materials/realia setup, gestures, movement, turn-taking, grouping, box/bag/knock/count/shake/blow/reveal routines, and cleanup when specified.
5. **Storytelling integrity is blocking.** Never infer a generic story flow. Preserve the exact source instruction for each Day: teacher vs student storytelling, exact page range, full-story instruction, grouping/pairs, approximate pages per group, narrator/character roles, prompts/gestures and support. Example for Emma Story 1: Day 1 pp.1-5; Day 2 pp.1-7; Day 3 pp.1-9; Day 4 pp.1-11; Day 5 pp.1-13; Day 6 pp.1-16; Days 7-8 student storytelling in pairs/groups of three, about four pages per group. Days 9-10 have no Story activity in the canonical lesson plan.
6. **Timing and order are source-authored.** Preserve every activity duration and sequence exactly. Do not normalize totals. Bonus activities remain bonus for Kids courses. An activity whose source title contains the word BONUS but is numbered inside the main lesson sequence remains core (e.g. Emma Story 1 Day 10 Worksheet 10).
7. **Audio/material specificity is retained.** Preserve exact TR numbers and activity-specific material/card codes where relevant to delivery.
8. **Validation before DONE.** A Day is FAIL if any source-required spoken phrase, routine/version, HOW TO PLAY stage, storytelling mode/page range/grouping, core activity, duration/order, or bonus classification is missing or contradicted. A 60-minute total alone is never proof of completeness.
9. **No completion claim without source comparison.** Canonical lessons are considered drafts until compared against the original Teacher Guide under this contract.
